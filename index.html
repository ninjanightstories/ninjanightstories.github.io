Ninja Night Stories Tools

Internal automation for the Ninja Night Stories family of channels.

This application is used only by the owner of Ninja Night Stories to manage their own accounts: tracking new YouTube uploads, adding support links (Patreon, Ko-fi) to the descriptions of their own videos, and publishing their own short story clips to their own Facebook Page, Instagram and TikTok accounts after manual approval.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's data.

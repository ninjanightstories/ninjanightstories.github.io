About the application

Ninja Night Stories Tools is a private automation application operated by the owner of the Ninja Night Stories channels. It is used only to publish and manage the owner's own original "What If" story videos on the owner's own accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Acceptable use

  • The application only acts on accounts that belong to the owner and that the owner has explicitly authorized.
  • Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is published.
  • The application does not post on behalf of other people and does not access other users' data.
